# Game Concept — The Next Stop

**Course:** SD5913 — Group Project 2026  
**Team:** Popping Minions  
**Status:** Concept Development

## 1. Game Overview

*The Next Stop* is an interactive narrative adventure game about a young person who believes that missing an important examination means losing their future.

While rushing to the examination venue, the protagonist boards a train that unexpectedly leads to several surreal worlds. Through unexpected encounters, alternative paths, and moments of failure, the protagonist gradually discovers that life does not have only one correct destination.

**Core Message:** Important opportunities deserve our effort, but they do not define our entire lives.

## 2. Target Audience

Young adults and students who experience academic pressure, uncertainty about the future, and anxiety about falling behind their peers.

## 3. Core Gameplay

The game combines narrative exploration with simplified 2.5D running mechanics and planned gesture-based interaction.

Planned player actions include:

- Moving left and right to change direction or avoid obstacles.
- Jumping over obstacles.
- Holding an open palm to interact with selected environmental objects.
- Making a forward gesture to take the final step in the epilogue.

Keyboard controls will be used during early prototyping before gesture recognition is integrated and tested.

**Core Design Principle: Failure is not the end.**

Instead of repeatedly restarting after mistakes, players may discover alternative routes and continue their journey.

## 4. Narrative Structure

**Prologue — Don't Be Late:** The protagonist rushes through a train station, believing that the examination is their only opportunity.

**Chapter 1 — The Standard Answer Avenue:** An endless examination-themed road introduces running mechanics and alternative paths after failure.

**Chapter 2 — Everyone's Own Pace:** A bicycle journey explores the idea that people have different destinations and priorities.

**Chapter 3 — After the Fall:** A failed jump leads to an unexpected route, where the protagonist helps another character repair a bridge.

**Chapter 4 — A River Without a Route:** A boat journey encourages the player to navigate uncertainty rather than control every outcome.

**Epilogue — The Next Step:** The protagonist returns to reality, discovers that the examination has been missed, and chooses to take a step forward.

## 5. Visual Direction

Although each chapter explores a different visual atmosphere, three elements remain consistent:

1. The protagonist's orange-red backpack.
2. A road or pathway extending toward the distance.
3. Recurring circular imagery, including clocks, wheels, tunnels, and ripples.

## 6. Initial Prototype Scope

The first playable prototype will focus on:

- A simplified running character.
- Basic movement and obstacle avoidance.
- Collision feedback.
- Transitioning to an alternative path after a mistake.
- Continuing gameplay without restarting.

Additional narrative scenes, visual assets, audio, and gesture controls will be introduced through later iterations.

## 7. Current Development Status

The team has established the initial narrative concept, gameplay direction, and visual guidelines.

Level concept artwork is currently being explored. The playable interaction and technical implementation are planned development tasks and have not yet been verified.
